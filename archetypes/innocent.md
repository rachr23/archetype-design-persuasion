# The Innocent Archetype

## 1. What is it?

The Innocent archetype represents simplicity, optimism, honesty, and goodness. Innocent brands try to make people feel safe, comfortable, and positive. Their communication is usually clear, friendly, and easy to understand.

The main goal of the Innocent archetype is to create happiness and trust through simple and positive experiences. Common characteristics include optimism, purity, honesty, simplicity, and reliability.

## 2. How do you recognize or use it?

The Innocent archetype works well for brands that want to appear trustworthy, wholesome, simple, or family-friendly. It can be used for products such as food, personal care, wellness, and other products where customers value simplicity and trust.

In design, Innocent brands often use clean layouts, natural or cheerful images, simple typography, and bright or soft colors. Their writing is usually positive, straightforward, and easy to understand.

## 3. Examples

### Dove

Dove is often associated with the Innocent archetype because its Real Beauty campaigns focus on natural beauty and positive self-image. The brand uses simple and sincere communication to create a welcoming and trustworthy feeling.

### Coca-Cola

Coca-Cola is another example often associated with the Innocent archetype. Its advertising has frequently focused on happiness, togetherness, and the simple enjoyment of a drink.
