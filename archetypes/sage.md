# The Sage Archetype

## 1. What is it?

The Sage archetype represents knowledge, truth, wisdom, and the desire to understand the world. Sage brands focus on providing reliable information and helping people make informed decisions.

The main goal of the Sage archetype is to seek truth and share knowledge. Common characteristics include intelligence, expertise, research, learning, and reliability.

## 2. How do you recognize or use it?

The Sage archetype works well for brands that provide information, education, research, or expert advice. It is commonly used by technology, education, media, and research organizations.

Sage brands often use clean and organized designs, informative language, and facts or evidence to build credibility. Their communication usually focuses on helping people learn and understand something.

## 3. Examples

### Google

Google can be associated with the Sage archetype because its search engine helps people find information and learn about different topics. Its products are built around organizing and providing access to information.

### Wikipedia

Wikipedia is another example that fits the Sage archetype because its main purpose is to provide information and knowledge about a wide range of subjects. Its website focuses on research, facts, and learning.
