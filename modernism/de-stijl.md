# De Stijl

## 1. What is it?

De Stijl was a Dutch modernist art and design movement that began in 1917. The movement focused on simplicity, order, and balance. Artists and designers used basic geometric forms and a limited color palette to create clear and organized compositions.

De Stijl is strongly associated with horizontal and vertical lines, rectangular shapes, and the use of primary colors such as red, blue, and yellow, along with black, white, and gray.

## 2. How do you recognize or use it?

De Stijl can be recognized by its straight horizontal and vertical lines, rectangular shapes, simple layouts, and limited colors. Designs often look balanced and organized rather than detailed or decorative.

The style can be used in posters, websites, branding, architecture, furniture, and other visual designs. Its simple structure can help organize information and create a clean visual appearance.

## 3. Examples

### Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow — Piet Mondrian

Piet Mondrian's paintings are among the best-known examples of De Stijl. His compositions use straight black lines, rectangular areas, and primary colors to create a carefully balanced design.

**Creator:** Piet Mondrian  
**Title:** Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow  
**Date:** 1930  
**Movement:** De Stijl

[Source: Museum of Modern Art — Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/79830)

### Rietveld Schröder House — Gerrit Rietveld

The Rietveld Schröder House in Utrecht, Netherlands, was designed by Gerrit Rietveld in 1924. Its geometric forms, horizontal and vertical elements, and use of color reflect important ideas associated with De Stijl.

**Creator:** Gerrit Rietveld  
**Title:** Rietveld Schröder House  
**Date:** 1924  
**Location:** Utrecht, Netherlands

[Source: UNESCO — Rietveld Schröderhuis](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/965/)
