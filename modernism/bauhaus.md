# Bauhaus

## 1. What is it?

Bauhaus is a modernist design movement that began in Germany in 1919. It was founded by architect Walter Gropius and brought together art, design, architecture, and craft. Bauhaus designers focused on combining creativity with practical function.

Bauhaus design is known for simplicity, geometric shapes, clean lines, and the idea that design should be useful as well as visually appealing.

## 2. How do you recognize or use it?

Bauhaus designs often use simple geometric shapes such as circles, squares, and triangles. They usually have clean layouts, minimal decoration, and clear typography. Primary colors such as red, blue, and yellow are also commonly associated with Bauhaus.

Bauhaus can be used for posters, websites, product design, architecture, advertisements, and branding. Its simple structure can make information easier to understand while giving a design a modern appearance.

## 3. Examples

### Bauhaus Building — Walter Gropius

The Bauhaus building in Dessau, Germany, was designed by Walter Gropius and completed in 1926. Its geometric forms, large windows, and functional structure demonstrate important ideas of Bauhaus architecture.

**Creator:** Walter Gropius  
**Title:** Bauhaus Building, Dessau  
**Date:** 1925–1926  
**Institution:** Bauhaus Dessau

[Source: Bauhaus Dessau](https://bauhaus-dessau.de/en/)

### Bauhaus Poster Design

Bauhaus posters often combined geometric shapes, typography, photography, and strong visual organization. These designs demonstrated the movement's interest in combining artistic expression with practical communication.

**Example:** Bauhaus exhibition and promotional posters  
**Date:** 1920s–1930s
