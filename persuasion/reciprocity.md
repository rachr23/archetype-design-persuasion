# Reciprocity

## 1. What is it?

Reciprocity is a principle of persuasion based on the idea that people often feel encouraged to return a favor when someone gives them something useful first.

In marketing and communication, reciprocity can be used by offering something valuable to an audience before asking them to take an action. The offer could be useful information, a free resource, a sample, or helpful advice.

## 2. How do you recognize or use it?

Reciprocity can be recognized when a person or organization provides something valuable without requiring an immediate purchase or commitment.

For example, a company might offer a free guide, tutorial, sample, or educational resource and then invite the audience to sign up for more information or explore a product.

The key is that the free offer should provide genuine value rather than misleading the audience.

## 3. Example

### Free Educational Guide

Imagine a career website offering a free resume guide. The guide gives students useful information about writing resumes and preparing for job applications. After providing the guide, the website may invite students to sign up for additional career resources.

This uses reciprocity because the audience receives something useful first, which can encourage them to engage further with the organization.

**Persuasion principle:** Reciprocity

**Application:** Give the audience a useful free resource before asking them to take another action.
